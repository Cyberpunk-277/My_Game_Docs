## Ranking notes

- **Disguises determine the ranking.** Areas / POIs are included as a separate comparison metric and do not affect placement.
- **Ties share the same rank.** The next rank follows the tied positions (for example, the three locations tied at rank 6 are followed by rank 9).
- **Counts are preserved as recorded.** No missing values have been estimated, and no additional locations or community rankings have been added.
