# Super Mario – Definitive Game Rankings & Modern Playing Guide (2026)

**Critics + Fans + Replayability + Modern Accessibility**

*Last updated: November 10, 2026*

The goal is to answer three questions for every core mainline Mario adventure:

1. How good is it compared with the other Mario games?
2. Why do critics and fans consider it worth playing?
3. What is the best way to experience it today?

This guide evaluates both historical greatness and present-day playability. A game can be an extraordinary achievement but still require inconvenient legacy hardware. Conversely, a modern release can make an older classic more appealing than ever.

---

# Ranking Methodology

This editorial framework evaluates mainline 2D and 3D Mario platformers, excluding karting, sports, party games, RPGs, mobile spin-offs and dedicated level-creation games.

| Ranking Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Critical reception (Metacritic and historical reviews) | 30% |
| Fan reception and community longevity | 30% |
| Level design, physics and movement innovation | 20% |
| Replayability and modern quality of life | 10% |
| Legacy and franchise influence | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

### How to Interpret the Rankings

- **Critical reception:** Original review consensus, historical reputation and contemporary reassessment.
- **Fan reception:** Long-term community sentiment, nostalgia, replay culture and enduring popularity.
- **Gameplay:** Movement mechanics, level design, creativity, pacing and technical execution.
- **Replayability:** Secrets, optional challenges, speedrunning, multiplayer and reasons to return.
- **Modern accessibility:** Available hardware, updated controls, resolution, portability and convenience.

Metacritic scores are included where meaningful, with the platform identified when relevant. Older games do not always have directly comparable review aggregates, so historical scores should not be treated as perfectly equivalent.

# 1. 3D Mario Games

*Ranked by combined critic and fan consensus, movement quality, level design, creativity and replayability.*

## #1 — Greatest Overall 3D Adventure

### Super Mario Galaxy (2007)

**Best modern version:** Super Mario Galaxy – Nintendo Switch (2025 release) / Nintendo Switch 2 with free enhancement update.

**Why it ranks here:** One of Nintendo's greatest creative achievements. Spherical gravity, imaginative planetary environments, spectacular orchestral music and exceptional visual presentation create an unforgettable platforming adventure.

**Fan perspective:** Frequently considered the emotional and atmospheric pinnacle of 3D Mario. Rosalina's storybook, the Lumas and the cosmic setting give it an identity unlike any other entry.

**Replayability & QoL:** Excellent pacing, collectible Power Stars, hidden challenges and a satisfying progression system. The updated Switch release improves resolution, interface, controls and adds Storybook content.

**Modern experience:** The 2025 Switch release supports enhanced resolution and improved button, stick and motion controls. Switch 2 supports 4K output following its free update.

**Original Metacritic score:** 97.

---

## #2 — Best Modern Open Sandbox

### Super Mario Odyssey (2017)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch / Nintendo Switch 2 backward compatibility.

**Why it ranks here:** A triumph of movement and exploration. Cappy's capture mechanic transforms enemies, creatures and objects into new ways to navigate the environment. Kingdoms are packed with secrets, vertical exploration and creative platforming.

**Fan perspective:** Widely celebrated as the spiritual successor to Super Mario 64. Its movement system has an exceptionally high skill ceiling, especially when players master advanced jumps and Cappy techniques.

**Replayability & QoL:** Enormous post-game content, hundreds of additional Power Moons, responsive controls and tremendous speedrunning potential. Some players criticize the number of relatively easy or repetitive moons.

**Modern experience:** Excellent on Switch and playable on Switch 2 through backward compatibility. No separate Switch 2 edition is required.

**Metacritic score:** 97.

---

## #3 — Best Pure Platforming Challenge

### Super Mario Galaxy 2 (2010)

**Best modern version:** Super Mario Galaxy 2 – Nintendo Switch (2025 release) / Nintendo Switch 2 with free enhancement update.

**Why it ranks here:** Takes the original Galaxy formula and pushes its platforming further. More demanding level design, creative power-ups, Yoshi mechanics and increasingly elaborate challenges make it arguably the strongest pure platforming entry.

**Fan perspective:** Many experienced players actually prefer Galaxy 2 to the original. Its level design is more mechanically focused, its difficulty is better developed and its challenge progression is exceptional.

**Replayability & QoL:** Excellent for players who want to master increasingly difficult stages. The later Green Star challenges provide additional reasons to revisit completed levels.

**Modern experience:** The 2025 Switch release finally eliminates the need for Wii hardware. It includes improved controls, interface, additional content and soundtrack functionality. Switch 2 adds 4K support.

**Original Metacritic score:** 97.

---

## #4 — The Foundational Masterpiece

### Super Mario 64 (1996)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack – Nintendo 64 Classics / Super Mario 3D All-Stars if already owned.

**Why it ranks here:** Established the fundamental language of 3D platforming. Analog movement, momentum, camera control, open-ended objectives and exploratory level design changed video games forever.

**Fan perspective:** A legendary speedrunning game with an incredibly high movement skill ceiling. Its influence is still visible in modern platformers.

**Replayability & QoL:** Incredible for movement enthusiasts and speedrunners. However, the original camera system can be frustrating for players accustomed to modern twin-stick controls.

**Modern experience:** The Nintendo 64 version is available through Switch Online + Expansion Pack. The 3D All-Stars version offers an alternative if you own it.

**Original Metacritic score:** 94.

---

## #5 — Best 3D Multiplayer Platformer

### Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury (2021)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch / Nintendo Switch 2 backward compatibility.

**Why it ranks here:** Combines tightly designed, obstacle-course-style 3D levels with four-player cooperative gameplay. The Cat Suit adds another dimension to movement, climbing and exploration.

**Fan perspective:** The original 3D World is a fantastic multiplayer experience. However, Bowser's Fury is arguably the most interesting part of the package from a single-player perspective.

**Replayability & QoL:** Excellent level variety, collectibles, character differences and multiplayer. The Switch version improves movement speed, adds online multiplayer and includes the complete Bowser's Fury adventure.

**Bowser's Fury:** A seamless open-world experiment that hints at where Nintendo could take future 3D Mario games.

**Metacritic score:** 89 (Switch version).

---

## #6 — Best Movement Depth and Atmosphere

### Super Mario Sunshine (2002)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo GameCube – Nintendo Classics on Switch 2 with Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack / Super Mario 3D All-Stars if already owned.

**Why it ranks here:** Isle Delfino is arguably the most cohesive and organic world in 3D Mario. FLUDD introduces jet-propulsion movement, water-based platforming, high-speed traversal and a unique approach to exploration.

**Fan perspective:** A divisive cult favorite with passionate defenders. Fans love its atmosphere, movement depth and interconnected setting. Critics often point to inconsistent mission design, blue coin hunting, camera problems and frustrating special stages.

**Replayability & QoL:** Extremely rewarding once mastered, but less forgiving than most other 3D entries.

**Modern experience:** A major improvement in 2026: Sunshine is now available in the GameCube Classics library on Switch 2. This requires the Expansion Pack subscription. The 3D All-Stars physical release remains an alternative.

**Original Metacritic score:** 92.

---

## #7 — Best Bite-Sized 3D Adventure

### Super Mario 3D Land (2011)

**Best modern version:** Original Nintendo 3DS physical cartridge.

**Why it ranks here:** Brilliantly combines 2D Mario obstacle-course design with 3D movement and stereoscopic depth. The levels are compact, inventive and designed around precise platforming.

**Fan perspective:** Sometimes overlooked because 3D World expanded its ideas considerably, but its Special Worlds offer some of the best handheld Mario challenges.

**Replayability & QoL:** Excellent pickup-and-play structure, hidden collectibles and post-game levels. Its main limitation is the hardware itself.

**Modern experience:** Still a 3DS game. The 3DS eShop closed in 2023, so new digital purchases are no longer possible. Physical cartridges remain the practical recommendation.

**Metacritic score:** 90.

# 2. 2D Mario Games

*Ranked across the NES, SNES, Game Boy, DS, Wii, New series and modern generations.*

## #1 — The Timeless Peak

### Super Mario World (1990)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online – Super Nintendo Classics.

**Why it ranks here:** A masterclass in pacing, visual design, exploration and secrets. The branching overworld, introduction of Yoshi, Cape Feather and Star World/Special Zone create one of the most satisfying platforming adventures ever designed.

**Fan perspective:** Frequently considered the greatest 2D Mario game. Its world design and secrets reward exploration without sacrificing accessibility.

**Replayability & QoL:** Exceptional. Multiple exits, secret paths, alternate progression and excellent movement make it endlessly replayable.

**Modern experience:** Excellent on Switch and Switch 2 through the SNES Classics library. Save states and rewind provide optional convenience.

**Historical significance:** One of the defining games of the 16-bit generation.

---

## #2 — The 8-Bit Masterwork

### Super Mario Bros. 3 (1988)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online – NES Classics / Super Mario All-Stars (SNES), if available through an owned copy.

**Why it ranks here:** Expanded 2D Mario into a massive adventure with themed worlds, inventory items, airships, transformation suits and elaborate stage concepts.

**Fan perspective:** Constantly debated against Super Mario World for the top 2D position. Its tighter movement and inventive power-ups remain beloved.

**Replayability & QoL:** Excellent. Different suits, world progression, secret whistles and alternative routes provide substantial replay value.

**Modern experience:** The NES version is available through standard Switch Online. The Super Mario All-Stars version provides enhanced 16-bit graphics if you already own a copy.

**Historical significance:** Established many of the conventions that defined 2D Mario for decades.

---

## #3 — Modern Reinvention

### Super Mario Bros. Wonder (2023)

**Best modern version:** Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park (2026).

**Why it ranks here:** Completely revitalized 2D Mario with expressive animation, excellent visual design, creative musical presentation and the unpredictable Wonder Flower mechanic.

**Fan perspective:** Widely praised as the freshest traditional 2D Mario experience in decades. Some veterans find the main campaign relatively easy, although the Special World provides substantially tougher challenges.

**Replayability & QoL:** Badge customization creates different movement possibilities and playstyles. Multiplayer and collectibles add additional value.

**Modern experience:** The 2026 Switch 2 Edition adds Bellabel Park, new multiplayer attractions, additional main-game content, Rosalina as a playable character and new challenges. Existing Switch owners can purchase the upgrade pack.

**Original Metacritic score:** 92.

---

## #4 — Master of Momentum

### New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe (2019 / original 2012)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch / Nintendo Switch 2 backward compatibility.

**Why it ranks here:** The gameplay peak of the New Super Mario Bros. formula. Excellent movement, Challenge Mode, tightly designed obstacle courses and the demanding New Super Luigi U expansion.

**Fan perspective:** Often criticized for aesthetic and musical repetition across the New series, but the underlying level design is exceptional.

**Replayability & QoL:** Bundles both U and Luigi U, supports multiplayer and provides considerable challenge content.

**Modern experience:** The Switch edition is the definitive convenient version. It is also compatible with Switch 2.

**Metacritic score:** 80 (Switch version) / 84 (Wii U original).

---

## #5 — The Game Boy Evolution

### Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins (1992)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online – Game Boy Classics.

**Why it ranks here:** Replaced the first Land game's unusual vehicle-shooter segments with a much larger and more traditional Mario adventure.

**Fan perspective:** A beloved handheld classic with distinctive themed worlds, excellent sprite work and the introduction of Wario.

**Replayability & QoL:** Multiple zones, secrets and approachable progression make it enjoyable to revisit. Rewind is particularly useful for its more awkward platforming sections.

**Modern experience:** Available through standard Nintendo Switch Online on Switch and Switch 2.

**Historical significance:** Helped establish the potential of substantial portable Mario adventures.

---

## #6 — The Industry Foundation

### Super Mario Bros. (1985)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online – NES Classics.

**Why it ranks here:** The game that helped establish Nintendo's dominance in the home console market and defined the side-scrolling platformer.

**Fan perspective:** World 1-1 remains one of the most influential examples of tutorial and level design in gaming history.

**Replayability & QoL:** Extremely replayable for arcade-style playthroughs, speedrunning and challenge runs. However, its movement and level design feel more restrictive than later games.

**Modern experience:** Easily accessible through standard Switch Online, including save states and rewind.

---

## #7 — The Revitalizing Reboot

### New Super Mario Bros. (DS, 2006)

**Best modern version:** Original Nintendo DS cartridge / compatible Nintendo 3DS hardware.

**Why it ranks here:** Revived traditional side-scrolling Mario after a lengthy absence from the home-console-style formula.

**Fan perspective:** A hugely influential commercial success that established the New series. Its clean level design remains enjoyable, even if later entries expanded its mechanics.

**Replayability & QoL:** Mega Mushrooms, multiplayer mini-games, Mario vs. Luigi and hidden Star Coins offer plenty of additional content.

**Modern experience:** Best experienced on original DS or compatible 3DS hardware. The 3DS and Wii U digital storefronts are closed.

**Metacritic score:** 89.

---

## #8 — Chaos Multiplayer Pioneer

### New Super Mario Bros. Wii (2009)

**Best modern version:** Original Wii / Wii U with an existing copy.

**Why it ranks here:** Introduced simultaneous four-player cooperative platforming to traditional console Mario. Added Propeller Suits, multiplayer challenges and Yoshi.

**Fan perspective:** An outstanding party-platformer. Multiplayer can become chaotic when players collide, but that chaos is part of its charm.

**Replayability & QoL:** Excellent with friends, particularly for collecting Star Coins and tackling difficult levels.

**Modern experience:** Requires Wii or Wii U hardware and an existing physical copy. Wii U is backward compatible with Wii discs.

**Metacritic score:** 87.

---

## #9 — The Coin-Rush Experiment

### New Super Mario Bros. 2 (2012)

**Best modern version:** Original Nintendo 3DS cartridge.

**Why it ranks here:** Built around an enormous coin-collecting concept, with Gold Flowers, Gold Rings and Coin Rush mode.

**Fan perspective:** Frequently considered the most formulaic New series entry. Its release close to New Super Mario Bros. U also contributed to franchise fatigue.

**Replayability & QoL:** Coin Rush and score chasing offer considerable replay value, but standard progression can feel repetitive.

**Modern experience:** Best played on original 3DS hardware with a physical cartridge. New eShop purchases are no longer possible.

**Metacritic score:** 78.

---

## #10 — The Quirky Curio

### Super Mario Bros. 2 (USA, 1988)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online – NES Classics.

**Why it ranks here:** Originally developed as Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic, this Western Mario sequel introduced a completely different gameplay formula based on picking up and throwing enemies and objects.

**Fan perspective:** Loved for its unusual mechanics, distinctive atmosphere and character selection. Luigi's high jumps and Peach's floating ability give the game substantial personality.

**Replayability & QoL:** Four playable characters with different abilities encourage experimentation and alternative playthroughs.

**Modern experience:** Available through standard Switch Online. Save states and rewind help with some of its more frustrating sections.

**Legacy:** Established Shy Guys, Bob-ombs and several other recognizable Mario characters.

---

## #11 — The Pocket Prototype

### Super Mario Land (1989)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online – Game Boy Classics.

**Why it ranks here:** Gunpei Yokoi's portable Mario adventure introduced Sarasaland and Princess Daisy, alongside unusual vehicle-based shooting levels.

**Fan perspective:** Charming and historically important, but comparatively primitive in movement and level design.

**Replayability & QoL:** A short, approximately 30–40-minute adventure with simple progression. Its unusual physics and small character sprites make it feel dated.

**Modern experience:** Available through standard Switch Online on Switch and Switch 2.

---

## #12 — The Sadistic Trial

### Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels (1986)

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch Online – NES Classics / Super Mario All-Stars if already owned.

**Why it ranks here:** The original Japanese sequel to Super Mario Bros. Designed for players who had mastered the original, it introduces poison mushrooms, wind effects, hidden traps and punishing level layouts.

**Fan perspective:** Respected for its difficulty but often criticized for relying on frustration rather than consistently rewarding challenge.

**Replayability & QoL:** Interesting for completionists and skilled platforming enthusiasts. NSO rewind and save states make it considerably more approachable.

**Modern experience:** Available through standard Switch Online. The All-Stars version offers a graphical alternative.

**Historical significance:** Demonstrates how far Nintendo was willing to push the difficulty of its original formula.

# 3. Creative & Level Creation Sandbox

*These games are fundamentally different from traditional Mario adventures. They deserve their own category because their primary value is creativity and potentially unlimited replayability.*

## The Infinite Toybox

### Super Mario Maker 2 (2019)

**Original platform:** Nintendo Switch.

**Best modern version:** Nintendo Switch / Nintendo Switch 2 backward compatibility.

**Why it stands out:** Combines the functional history of 2D Mario into one creation platform. Players can build levels using Super Mario Bros., Super Mario Bros. 3, Super Mario World, New Super Mario Bros. U and Super Mario 3D World styles.

**Fan perspective:** One of the best value propositions in the franchise for players who enjoy creative experimentation. The community has produced everything from traditional Nintendo-style levels to extraordinarily difficult technical challenges.

**Replayability:** Nearly unlimited through community-created courses, Endless Challenge and the built-in 100+ level Story Mode.

**Best way to play:** Nintendo Switch with an active Nintendo Switch Online membership for downloading and sharing courses.

**Modern experience:** Switch 2 backward compatibility provides another way to play. Nintendo lists the game as supported.

**Important distinction:** Online community features require Nintendo Switch Online. The built-in Story Mode and course creation remain valuable without relying on community content.

## Important Modern Hardware Notes

### Nintendo Switch Online – Standard Membership

Includes access to the NES, SNES and Game Boy Classics libraries.

**Useful for:**

- Super Mario Bros.
- Super Mario Bros. 2
- Super Mario Bros. 3
- The Lost Levels
- Super Mario World
- Super Mario Land
- Super Mario Land 2

### Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack

Adds the Nintendo 64, Game Boy Advance, Sega Genesis, Virtual Boy and other supported classic libraries.

**Useful for:**

- Super Mario 64 through Nintendo 64 Classics.

### Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack on Switch 2

Adds access to the Nintendo GameCube Classics library, which includes:

- Super Mario Sunshine.
- Other GameCube classics.

This is particularly important because Sunshine previously required either an original GameCube or an existing copy of Super Mario 3D All-Stars to play on modern Nintendo hardware.

### Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 (2025)

Both games received dedicated Switch releases. The bundle is available physically, while the individual games are also available digitally.

**Switch 2 offers:**

- 4K resolution on supported displays.
- Improved visuals.
- Updated controls.
- Improved user interface.
- Additional Storybook content.
- Soundtrack functionality.

### Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Switch 2 Edition (2026)

The Switch 2 Edition adds:

- Meetup in Bellabel Park.
- New multiplayer attractions.
- Additional main-game content.
- Rosalina as a playable character.
- New challenges and features.

Existing owners of the original Switch game can purchase the upgrade pack.

### Super Mario 3D All-Stars Availability

The original collection included Super Mario 64, Sunshine and Galaxy.

However:

- Digital sales ended March 31, 2021.
- Previously purchased digital copies remain playable and redownloadable.
- Physical copies can still be acquired through resale markets.
- It is no longer the best default recommendation for any of the three games now that better individual access options exist.

### Legacy Hardware Warning

The Nintendo 3DS and Wii U eShops closed to new purchases in March 2023. Their online services ended in April 2024.

Previously purchased software can still be redownloaded, and physical cartridges and discs remain usable.

For new players, this makes Nintendo Switch Online a particularly valuable alternative for the older 2D titles.

# 6. My Recommended Mario Play Order

For someone who wants to experience the best of the franchise without necessarily playing every entry chronologically, this is my recommended order.

## Best 3D Mario Journey

1. **Super Mario Odyssey** – Start with the most accessible modern sandbox.
2. **Super Mario Galaxy** – Experience Nintendo's greatest combination of atmosphere and platforming.
3. **Super Mario Galaxy 2** – Continue with the more demanding, mechanically focused sequel.
4. **Super Mario 64** – Appreciate the origins of 3D Mario movement.
5. **Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury** – Enjoy cooperative platforming and open-world experimentation.
6. **Super Mario Sunshine** – Finish with the most divisive but mechanically interesting adventure.
7. **Super Mario 3D Land** – A worthwhile handheld bonus.

## Best 2D Mario Journey

1. **Super Mario Bros. 3** – Understand the classic formula.
2. **Super Mario World** – Experience its most refined traditional form.
3. **Super Mario Bros. Wonder** – See how Nintendo reinvented the formula.
4. **New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe** – Explore the modern traditional approach.
5. **Super Mario Bros. (1985)** – Appreciate the historical foundation.
6. **Super Mario Land 2** – Explore the handheld evolution.
7. **The remaining New Super Mario Bros. entries** – Experience the different multiplayer and handheld variations.

## If You Only Play Five Mario Games

| Priority | Game | Why |
|---:|---|---|

| 2 | Super Mario Odyssey | The most complete modern 3D sandbox |
| 3 | Super Mario World | The definitive traditional 2D adventure |

| 5 | Super Mario Bros. Wonder | The freshest modern 2D experience |

# Final Verdict

Mario's greatest strength is how consistently Nintendo reinvents the platforming formula without losing its identity.

The 3D games showcase the franchise's greatest mechanical innovation, with Galaxy and Odyssey representing two different peaks. The 2D games demonstrate remarkable longevity, with Super Mario World and Super Mario Bros. 3 still holding their ground against modern releases.

The 2025 Galaxy rereleases and 2026 Switch 2 enhancements have also significantly improved the franchise's modern accessibility.

**My overall recommendation:** If you want to experience the absolute best of Mario today, start with **Super Mario Galaxy, Super Mario Odyssey, Super Mario Galaxy 2, Super Mario World and Super Mario Bros. Wonder.**

Together, they represent the strongest combination of historical importance, critical acclaim, fan appreciation and modern playability.
